# The Steam store page — what is left to prepare

The working plan for the rest of STEAM.md §9.2 (package S4) that needs no Steamworks account: the
texts, the icon, the description's loops and the event art. Written 2026-11-06, to be done on
the user's gaming PC in the next session; retired into the ARCHIVE once built, as SPECIAL_TOWERS.md
will be. The decisions stay in STEAM.md; this file gathers what the work needs.

## 1. What holds already (STEAM.md §1, §9.2)

- **Rampart is named nowhere on Steam**: not in the descriptions, the tags, the art, nor a
  disclaimer. The "inspired by" line stays in the game, the README and the release notes. (The
  YouTube trailer's description names it in the disclaimer; that text is not reused here.)
- **No links in the description**, and none implied (Valve's rule too, §2).
- **English and German** are the declared languages; Hessian stays an in-game joke.
- **Free**, the whole game, every style, no DLC.
- **Screenshots only of play**; no AI content ships in the game (the content survey).
- **The Steam build has only Steam's online services**: Steam lobbies (Public, Friends, Invite
  only; Friends the default), no link, no UPnP, no LAN. The texts must not promise those.

Sources: <https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/assets> and its pages `standard`,
`libraryassets`, `community` and `eventassets`. Since August 2024 Valve takes only the larger
capsule sizes; ours are those.

- **The shortcut icon**: "any alpha will be replaced with solid black", so it is designed on a
  solid ground, or on black. Steam makes the Windows ICO from a PNG. A Linux icon field was not
  found; the Mac field is irrelevant (no macOS).
- **The App icon** shows on the store page only from Coming Soon on.
- **Capsules**: no text but the title (ours comply).
- **Screenshots** (summary): at least five, four or more suitable for all ages. To confirm.

**The descriptions** (<https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/page/description>):

- **Short description**: "a few hundred characters" (no number given; commonly 300, to check in
  the form), **plain text only**, and nothing time-related ("coming soon", "out now").
- **About This Game**: text with headings and bold, and embedded images or looping GIFs; each
  image under **5 MB**, all of them together under **15 MB**. MP4 or WebM in the description are
  not mentioned there (the trailer page, <https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/trailer>, was
  not read). No recommended width is given; the description column is commonly said to be 616 px
  wide — to check on the live page.
- **Text in images must be translated too**, so the loops carry no captions.
- **No links**, no QR codes, no implied URLs; **no imitation of Steam's interface** (fake
  wishlist or buy buttons, price overlays); no advertising of other games.

**Tags** (<https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/tags>): at least 5 before launch, up to 20
recommended — only the top 20 show and steer visibility. Set by the developer in the Tag Wizard
(Edit Store Page → Basic Info), and by players and moderators.

**Languages** (<https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/localization/languages>): declared per
game in the store page's settings; English is Steam's fallback and must have content.

**Not found or not read**: the store page's legal-notice field (to see in the form), the
graphical asset rules (`/doc/store/assets/rules`), the trailer page, the localisation page.

## 3. The facts the texts draw on

Checked against the code and the locales on 2026-11-06. German names are the game's own, from
`config/locale/de.json`.

- **The game**: shoot down your opponents' castle walls, then race to rebuild your own before
  the next barrage. Cannon phase, combat, build phase; a wall must turn its corners; cannons are
  dead outside sealed ground; fail to seal a castle and a life is spent, and a fresh castle and
  ring come back. Ground and guns score. Ten rounds and three lives by default.
- **2 to 8 players**; every match a team match (free-for-all is teams of one), teams of two
  among them; teammates may build on each other's islands.
- **Bots at ten levels** (Stufe), any seat; a person can play alone against them.
- **Online**: on Steam, Steam lobbies — Public in the games list, Friends, Invite only; Steam
  names and avatars, Join from the friends list. The host can remove a guest in the lobby.
- **Special towers** (Spezialtürme), a table's switch, off by default — the classic game
  without: bought in the cannon phase for 2 or 3 cannons. Nine:

  | English             | German             |
  | ------------------- | ------------------ |
  | Big Bertha          | Dicke Berta        |
  | Architect           | Baumeister         |
  | Rapid Cannon        | Schnellfeuerkanone |
  | Lightning Tower     | Blitzturm          |
  | Allerdickste Bertha | Allerdickste Berta |
  | Laser Tower         | Laserturm          |
  | Frost Cannon        | Frostkanone        |
  | Lullaby Tower       | Schlafturm         |
  | Subwoofer           | Subwoofer          |

- **Tournaments** (Turniere): the player's team of people and bots against a field of named bot
  teams, a league and then a single- or double-elimination knockout, saved between matches;
  friends can join the player's team.
- **24 visual styles** (Optiken), chosen separately for building and combat, a new one every round
  if wished, a change of look sweeping across the board mid-match; each with its own music but
  three. English / German: Minimal, Medieval / Mittelalter, Night / Nacht, Cyberpunk, Blueprint /
  Blaupause, Parchment / Pergament, Toy bricks / Spielzeugsteine, Stained glass / Buntglas,
  Chocolate / Schokolade, Halloween, Sakura, Oktoberfest, Opera, Office / Büro, Under the sea /
  Unter dem Meer, Electric / Elektrisch, Cartoon / Zeichentrick, Christmas / Weihnachten, Noir,
  Stadium / Stadion, Pirates / Piraten, Anime, Mythos, Wild West / Wilder Westen.
- **Controls**: the mouse — two buttons and the wheel — or a controller through the same actions,
  with the Steam Deck in mind (STEAM.md §8). No keyboard needed.
- **Languages**: English and German, interface and texts; no voice.
- **Audio**: all from OpenGameArt.org and Pixabay, credited in the game.

**For the system requirements** (to complete by measuring the Steam build):

- Electron 44, so **Windows 10 or later, 64-bit** (Electron dropped 7 and 8 long ago); Linux
  64-bit under the Steam Linux Runtime.
- **Disk**: the unpacked Windows app was 415 MB (`packages/desktop/release/win-unpacked`,
  2026-11-03); the portable file 137 MB. Say 500 MB.
- **Graphics**: 58–60 fps at eight players on an integrated GPU (AMD Renoir; ARCHIVE 12n), so
  any GPU with WebGL 2 of the last years; a dedicated card not needed.
- **Memory**: the page took 356 MB in an eight-player match (ARCHIVE 14b); Electron's other
  processes come on top — to measure the whole app in Task Manager during an eight-player match.
- **Network**: broadband for online play; none for play against bots.
- The Deck: STEAM.md §8, its review requested at S5.

## 4. The work, in order

### 4.1 The icon (first: it blocks Steam, and the desktop app has none)

The desktop app has **no icon of its own** — `packages/desktop/package.json`'s `build` names none
and there is no `build/icon.*`, so the `.exe`, the AppImage and the window show Electron's. One
icon for all of them:

- **Design first, with the user**: candidates sketched, chosen as the capsules were. Ideas: the
  rainbow lettering's **B** on a dark tile; a castle in its wall ring with a cannon, from a style
  (Medieval, as on the capsules); a crowned main castle. It must read at 32 px and at 184 px, on
  a solid ground (Valve blacks out alpha).
- **Rendered by `tools/storeart`**, as the capsules are, so it can be made again: a new asset at
  512 × 512 PNG for Steam; 256 × 256 or larger for electron-builder (`build/icon.png` in
  `packages/desktop`, or `win.icon` / `linux.icon` in its config), which makes the ICO and the
  AppImage's icons; the window's icon set in `BrowserWindow` if it does not follow.
- Then the GitHub builds carry it too, from the next release.

### 4.3 The store's settings

To have ready to type in:

- **Tags** (up to 20, from Steam's own list — the Tag Wizard offers only its own). Candidates:
  Strategy, Multiplayer, Online PvP, PvP, Team-Based, Party Game, Casual, Action, Real-Time,
  Arcade, Retro, Base Building, Destruction, Artillery, Medieval, Colorful, Stylized, 2D,
  Controller, Free to Play (if a game free without purchases may use it); Castles if the list has

  one), Pixel Graphics (only some styles). To choose against the Wizard's list.
- **Genre**: Strategy, Action, Casual; **Free** as the price.
- **Features** (categories): Multi-player, Online PvP, Single-player (against bots — to decide
  whether that is honest to claim), Full controller support (once the C package is checked on the
  Steam build), Steam Deck later by review. No achievements, no cloud at launch (STEAM.md §10).
- **Supported languages**: English and German — interface yes, subtitles yes, full audio no.
- **System requirements**: §3.
- **The content survey** and **the IARC questionnaire**, answers drafted: cartoon violence against
  walls and cannons only, no people hurt (Anime's heroes and the sportsmen are guns of their
  style — note them), no blood, no language, no gambling, no purchases, online interaction
  without chat (there is no chat), no AI-generated content in the game.
- **The legal notice**, if the form has one: © 2026 Thomas Arnold; audio credited in the game.
  No Rampart (§1).

### 4.4 The description's loops

Three or four short loops, **no captions**, each under 5 MB and all under 15 MB, at the column's
width (616 px to check). Cut from the **raw clips on the gaming PC** (`trailer-build\half\`, kept
beside the trailer), not from the finished trailer, which carries captions. Candidates, one to a
section of §4.2:

- a change of look sweeping across the board (the Medieval → Cyberpunk wipe);
- a castle sealed — Cartoon's castle dancing, or Pirates' Jolly Roger going up;
- the eight-player pull-out (for Play together);
- a special tower's effect — the Lightning chain, or the Bertha's crater.

With ffmpeg's two-pass palette (`palettegen` then `paletteuse`), a few seconds each, 15–25 frames
a second; if a GIF will not fit, fewer frames or a narrower crop. Check whether Steam takes WebM or
MP4 in the description (the trailer page); they would be far smaller.
